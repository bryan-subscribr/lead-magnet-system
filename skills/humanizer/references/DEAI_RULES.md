# De-AI rules for Bryan Ng's content (zero tolerance)

Bryan's order (2026-10-09): no AI-specific tells anywhere in the content. Not "fewer". Zero.

## Banned (every instance must go)

1. **Negative parallelism / reframes.** Any "It's not X, it's Y", "X isn't A. It's B.", "X, not Y" as a reveal, "not because X, but because Y", "The question isn't X. The question is Y.", chiasmus ("Your audience didn't leave. Your consistency did.", "You stop chasing talent. Talent shows up."), "X wasn't the fix. Y was." Rewrite as a plain positive statement of the point. A literal factual contrast inside a list item that is genuinely needed ("aimed at buyers rather than viewers") may stay only if phrased plainly and it is the only one in the piece; prefer removing it.
2. **"Not X. Not Y. Just Z."** countdowns, including "No fancy editing. No camera skills. Just the words."
3. **"The X? Y."** self-asked questions answered right away ("The result? Devastating.", "Too simple? Here's...", "The last 12 months? I still can't believe them."). Real questions to the reader at the end (engagement CTA like "What's yours? 👇") are fine.
4. **"Here's the thing / Here's what / Here's how / Here's why / Here's what that taught me / Here's what we did"** and other false-suspense transitions ("And that's when it clicked", "That was the whole bet", "Read this one slowly.", "Let that sink in", "Here's the truth", "Plot twist").
5. **Magic adverbs and AI vocab:** quietly, deeply, fundamentally, remarkably, arguably, genuinely, truly, incredibly, certainly, utilize, leverage (verb), robust, streamline, harness, delve, unlock, elevate, seamless, game-changer, landscape, tapestry, paradigm, synergy, ecosystem, journey (as metaphor), "serves as", "stands as", "marks a", "represents", "In today's fast-paced world", "secret sauce", "revolutionary".
6. **Tricolon stacking.** Max one rule-of-three per piece, and only if the three items are real facts. No back-to-back triads like "They saw the business. They met my friends. And we got our relationship back." Rewrite into a normal sentence.
7. **Anaphora** (3+ sentences starting the same way in a row) outside a numbered list.
8. **Short punchy fragment spam.** No standalone fragment paragraphs for manufactured emphasis ("Small thing.", "Ever.", "Just the words.", "Nothing fancy.", "Same words. Roughly 9 times the views."). Group related sentences into normal paragraphs of 2-4 sentences. A one-line hook at the top and a one-line CTA at the bottom are fine. Real dialogue/quotes are fine.
9. **False vulnerability:** "This one was uncomfortable to write. That's how I knew I had to post it", "You probably needed to read one of these today", "And yes, I'll admit...". Cut it or replace with the plain fact.
10. **Fake-deep aphorisms / generic motivation** that add no information: "Decide who you're becoming. Then act like him on the boring days.", "It's never too late.", "You don't have to win the argument. You have to build the life.", "Proof ends arguments words can't.", "Hype pays you once. Things that compound keep paying you." Cut, or replace with the concrete thing it was gesturing at (only if that concrete thing is already in the text).
11. **Grandiose stakes:** "the decision that changed my life", "changed everything", "the most important thing". Say what actually happened.
12. **"Think of it as / It's like a..."** analogies, "Imagine..." openers, "Let's break this down / unpack / dive in".
13. **Vague attributions:** "experts say", "studies show" without a name.
14. **Invented concept labels** ("the comparison trap", "the follow-up problem" used as a term).
15. **Fractal summaries:** a closing line that just restates the post ("That's YouTube for business done as lead generation.", "Never treat your call to action as an afterthought." after a post about exactly that). Cut it.
16. **Formatting:** no em dashes (—), no en dashes used as dashes, no unicode arrows (→ ⟶ ➡) and no "->" arrows either. Lists use "- " or "1." Straight quotes only (" and '), never curly. No bold.
17. **Dead metaphors / repeated metaphors.**
18. **Superficial -ing analysis tails** ("...highlighting how much it matters").
